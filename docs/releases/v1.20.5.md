# v1.20.5 — 2026-10-07

j-space grows two modules, vendored from upstream's SV1 architectural
upgrade ([J-Space Cognition Suite](https://github.com/Tiger3807861189/J-Space-Cognition-Suite-V3.6),
Apache-2.0). Content-only change — no runtime behavior touched.

## Why these two

The full-delta post-mortems keep naming the same failure modes —
claims accepted without a recorded source, partial coverage reported
as universal clearance, consensus treated as evidence, security-flavored
guesses reported as findings. SV1's two new modules are protocols for
exactly that:

- **`modules/epistemics.md`** — evidence vs inference vs uncertainty,
  kept visible: the four knowledge quadrants as a search discipline,
  observed/inferred/unresolved separated in reports, attention spent
  on the highest-cost unknown, anomalies preserved as residuals
  instead of deleted.
- **`modules/cyber.md`** — authorized security work turned into
  reproducible claims: fix the scope, trace a violated property
  through the actual source, make the hypothesis falsifiable with a
  discriminating negative control, dispose findings as
  candidate/confirmed/rejected/fixed with explicit evidence
  obligations. Pairs with the h5i websec/recon plugins.

## What was deliberately left out

SV1's executable controller (`control.py`, `host_bridge.py`) and its
orchestration/repository modules are NOT vendored: their roles —
persistent gates, work/ship evidence checks, agent rounds and reviews —
are covered natively by the harness, and enforced rather than
voluntary (verify gates, delegation review, todos, context
management). Dead controller commands and cross-references in the two
vendored modules are mapped to harness equivalents (`council.vote`,
delegated review, `code.symbols`/`fs.*`, workspace evidence files) —
adaptations listed in `skills/j-space/NOTICE`.

SV1's one structurally new idea for us — a persistent, freshness-checked
semantic repo map per project — is filed in `ToDos_for_later.md` as a
possible future feature.
